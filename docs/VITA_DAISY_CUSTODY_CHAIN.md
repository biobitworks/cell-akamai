Its development targets are mechanical; biological provenance is not established. It is distinct from Pythia causal language models and from a VCC expression predictor.

The CPU successor is an explicitly bounded eight-step optimizer experiment on frozen development rows. Its preregistration records the parent checkpoint, view hash, seed, schedule, device and learning rate. Every step saves a checkpoint and SHA-256 receipt. Merkle leaves bind ordered step/validation receipts; this experiment-local tree is separate from canonical project MMR/SeedGraph anchoring. Predictions are saved and committed before deterministic scoring. Raw predictions/checkpoints remain private.

Before/after development validation is descriptive, not independent VCC improvement evidence.
